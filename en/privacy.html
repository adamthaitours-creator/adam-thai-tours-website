Privacy policy

We respect your privacy. This page explains in plain language what the website collects, why, and how we handle it.

Who we are

Adam Thai Tours is a travel agency arranging flights, hotels, packages and group tours. Contact us on WhatsApp +972 58 764 3159 or by email at Adam.thai.tours@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • What you type into the website's forms when you press "send": your name, phone number, email address and trip details (destination, dates, number of travellers, budget, notes).
  • We do not use advertising cookies, tracking or analytics tools.

Where it is stored and how it is sent

  • When you press a send button, a copy of your request is saved with our website host (Netlify) so it isn't lost and we can reply to you.
  • The request itself is sent by WhatsApp or email, as you choose; those services have their own privacy policies.
  • Our host may log general technical data (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the site, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and prepare options and quotes.
  • To contact you about your trip and booking.

We won't send you marketing messages without your consent.

Sharing

We never sell your information. We share it only as far as needed to complete your booking — with airlines, hotels, and insurance or transfer providers.

How long we keep it

We keep your request for as long as needed to handle it and meet any legal obligation, and delete it when you ask unless the law prevents it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. Message us on WhatsApp or email above and we'll take care of it.
